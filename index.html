Your Cosmic Journey

Mercury

The swift planet. A year lasts just 88 Earth days. Temperatures swing from -173°C to 427°C.

Venus

Earth's toxic twin. Shrouded in clouds of sulfuric acid with crushing pressure.

Earth

The pale blue dot. Our only home, for now. 71% covered in life-giving water.

Mars

The red planet. Home to the tallest volcano in the solar system: Olympus Mons.

Jupiter

King of planets. Its Great Red Spot is a storm larger than Earth, raging for 400+ years.

Saturn

Lord of the rings. Its rings are made of ice and rock, some as small as dust.

Voyager 1

Now 15+ billion miles away. Carrying a golden record of Earth into interstellar space.

Apollo 11

1969 moon landing. "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind."

Hubble Space Telescope

Launched in 1990. Revolutionized our understanding of the universe with stunning images.

Mars Rover Curiosity

Exploring the red planet since 2012. Searching for signs of ancient life.

International Space Station

Continuous human presence in space since 1998. A symbol of international cooperation.

James Webb Space Telescope

Launched in 2021. Observing the earliest galaxies and exoplanets in infrared.
